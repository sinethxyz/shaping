# Origin and status

## The proposal

On **10 October 2026**, **Sineth Madduma** publicly presented **SHAPING** as a creative practice concerned with the development of machine intelligence.

The working definition was:

> SHAPING is the creative practice of developing machine intelligence through intention, examples, experimentation and refinement.

The accompanying contrast was:

> A programmer works with instructions.  
> A designer works with visual and interactive elements.  
> A shaper works with intelligence itself.

A further formulation introduced the transition from **“Tell the machine how”** (programming), through **“Tell the machine what to build”** (AI-assisted or vibe coding), to **“Tell intelligence what to become”** (shaping).

This repository gathers and refines that **specific proposed framing** in a durable, versioned form.

## Important limits to the claim

- The ordinary English words *shaping* and *shaper* long predate this proposal.
- Technical approaches involving model shaping, reward shaping, fine-tuning, and iterative AI development also predate it.
- A dated post and a repository commit can document a public proposal; neither proves exclusive originality, inventorship of underlying techniques, or a legal monopoly over a word.
- The proposal is not yet a broadly accepted scientific, professional, or industry standard.

The intended contribution is to identify and develop a coherent **human creative activity** around machine intelligence, accessible beyond specialists while retaining evidence and technical accountability.

## Relationship to modelparadox

This language emerged while considering a long-term product direction for [modelparadox](https://modelparadox.com): a creative environment where people develop persistent machine-intelligence Creations, supported by advanced infrastructure.

The open **shaping** concept is broader than one product. This repository describes the activity; it is not a commitment that any particular modelparadox feature has already been implemented.
